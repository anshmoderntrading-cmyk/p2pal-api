Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how P2Pal (“P2Pal”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the P2Pal mobile application (the “App”). P2Pal is a non-custodial USDT wallet: you alone control your keys and funds. We designed the App to collect as little information as possible.

In short: Your recovery phrase and private keys never leave your device and are never sent to us. We cannot access, move, freeze or recover your funds.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following is generated and stored locally on your phone, inside the device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). It is never transmitted to P2Pal or any third party:

  • Your 12-word recovery phrase and the private keys derived from it
  • Your PIN (stored only as a salted, irreversible hash)
  • App preferences such as auto-lock timing and biometric settings

2. Information we process to operate the App

To show balances, build transactions and enable gas-free transfers, the App and our backend process the following. This is public blockchain data and does not identify you personally:

  • Your public wallet addresses (TRC20 and BEP20)
  • USDT balances and transaction history read from public blockchain explorers
  • For gas-free transfers: the public address, network and amount, together with a cryptographic signature that proves ownership of the sending address

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, government ID or bank details for use of the wallet itself.

3. Push notifications

If you enable notifications, we store a device push token together with your public wallet addresses so we can alert you when USDT arrives. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings.

4. Third-party services (buying & selling USDT)

When you choose to buy or sell USDT (“on-ramp / off-ramp” or “P2P”), the transaction is carried out by independent licensed third-party providers such as Transak or Onramp.money. These providers operate their own platforms and collect their own information, including any KYC/identity verification and payment details, under their own privacy policies. P2Pal never receives or stores your payment or identity information. Please review the provider’s privacy policy before using their service.

5. Service providers we rely on

The App communicates with infrastructure providers purely to read blockchain data and deliver notifications, for example RPC and explorer providers (such as Alchemy, TronGrid and Etherscan) and push-notification services. They receive only public addresses and technical request data.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not hold, control or have access to your funds.
  • We do not run advertising trackers inside the App.

7. Data retention

Device data stays until you remove the wallet from the App or uninstall it. Push-notification records are deleted when you disable notifications or the token expires.

8. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Security

Keys are held in your device’s hardware-backed secure storage, the recovery-phrase screen blocks screenshots, and the App is protected by a PIN and optional biometrics. However, no system is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for safeguarding your recovery phrase and device.

10. Your choices

You can disable notifications, turn off biometrics, or remove your wallet at any time. Because we do not hold personal accounts, there is no server-side profile for us to delete.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

12. Contact

Questions about this Policy: support@p2palwallet.online
